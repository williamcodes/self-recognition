# Where the Tide Stops · Producer sheet

Run time **3:00** from the paper opening to the end of the room-tone tail. Play the two parts close to the mic. The piece uses voices, Foley and one mono sound cue. There is no music.

Open [the performance script](01%20-%20Performance%20script.md) for both performers. Load [Tide at platform four](03%20-%20Tide%20at%20platform%20four.wav) onto one playback pad or track. It plays once from **1:21 to 1:47**. The other sounds can be made at a Foley station or recorded individually with the same props.

## Set up

- Give Mara a folded sheet and a shallow metal tin with a snug lid, two small clean pebbles and a spoonful of water in a cup. During the handoff, tilt the closed tin just enough to move the water and one pebble. Try it over a towel first.
- Use a clicky hand lamp or a mechanical switch for Ivo. With one mic, keep both actors at a consistent distance and angle the Foley across the mic so the tin taps stay below speech.
- Start with a few seconds of clean studio room tone on hand for the edit. In a live run, leave the voice mics open through the quiet beats.

## Call the cues

Times are checkpoints. Use the line or action to follow the cast. Cue D is the main timing anchor.

| Cue | Target | Trigger and sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 0:00 | On the start signal, unfold the paper in one clear movement. Ivo comes in as it settles. |
| B | 1:08 | After Ivo asks to see the bottom, turn the sheet. Mara reads the instruction. |
| C | 1:19 | After “The lamp still works,” click the switch on. Give the click its own space. |
| D | 1:21 | Start the tide file after the click. Let the low rumble turn into the first wash, about six seconds into the file, before Ivo says “Get back.” Ride the cue down under the voices. Let the second wash recede by Mara’s “There.” The file tails out on its own. |
| E | 1:51 | After “Just where the water went out,” open the tin and let the two pebbles tap inside. |
| F | 2:00 | After “Hold the light lower,” tip a small spoonful of water into the tin and seat the lid. |
| G | 2:16 | After “Yes. Put it out,” click the switch off. Hold the quiet until Ivo speaks. |
| H | 2:24 | After “Keep it level,” pass the tin and tilt it once. Give Ivo a moment to hear it. |
| I | 2:55 | After “My hands are shaking,” take back the tin and tilt it again. Let the tiny wash settle and carry the room tone to 3:00. |

For live Foley in place of D, rub a felt pad slowly along a metal tray for the opening rail sound. Bring up a slow back-and-forth wash in a shallow pan of water and clean gravel, starting about six seconds after the rail sound begins. Make a second, smaller wash about 16 seconds in. Ease to quiet by 1:47. Keep the tin at a separate mic position or bring it closer so its first taps read as a new sound.

## Rehearse to the slot

Run the opening exchange and the platform history through 0:55 as a flow of questions and answers. Leave time for the sound before Ivo’s warning and for both performers to hear the tin at the end. Ivo’s first warning is instinctive. Mara listens for the pebbles before reaching for them. Play the last exchange as an ordinary invitation.

On a stopwatch pass, aim to land the last line by 2:55. If the read runs long, tighten the early conversational gaps first. If it runs short, give the washes and handoff more time. Keep the final tin movement audible before the cut.
