# Where the Tide Stops

An audio drama for two performers. Target running time 3:00.

- **MARA** brings an old timetable and a tobacco tin. Her father can no longer get down to the shore.
- **IVO** used to work this station. He still has the hand lamp he used on the platform.

*Only names in bold introduce spoken lines. Times are rehearsal marks. Sound in brackets is cued by the producer.*

### 0:00–0:27

*[A. A large sheet of paper unfolds, close.]*

**IVO**  
You could have brought that in the morning.

**MARA**  
It says eleven fifty-eight. I thought we ought to be here when it says.

**IVO**  
Where did you find it?

**MARA**  
Behind Dad’s kitchen clock. See the thumbtack holes?

**IVO**  
I put those there. We pinned it to the booking-office door thirty years ago.

### 0:27–0:55

**MARA**  
What’s the little four beside the time?

**IVO**  
Platform four.

**MARA**  
There are only three.

**IVO**  
Four was down those steps. You could feel the boards lift when the tide came in. We filled the steps with concrete when they built the sea wall.

**MARA**  
Dad says he used to take his boots off down there. The nurse thought he meant the bath.

*[Give Ivo a beat to take that in.]*

### 0:55–1:21

**IVO**  
What’s in the tin?

**MARA**  
Nothing yet. I thought there might be a stone left. He taught me to listen for the little ones rolling back down the beach.

**IVO**  
Let me see the bottom of that.

*[B. Paper turns.]*

**MARA**  
“Request stop. Show a white light.”

**IVO**  
The lamp still works.

*[C. A switch clicks. A beat.]*

### 1:21–1:51

*[D. A low rail hum swells and gives way to water rolling over shingle. Listen for the first wash before Ivo speaks.]*

**IVO**  
Get back. That’s the edge.

**MARA**  
That was solid concrete.

**IVO**  
There’s one under your foot now. Hold my sleeve.

**MARA**  
Is there a train?

**IVO**  
I don’t see one. Keep hold.

*[The wash recedes. Mara takes in what’s within reach.]*

**MARA**  
There. Just where the water went out.

### 1:51–2:18

*[E. The tin opens. Two small pebbles tap inside.]*

**IVO**  
You got one?

**MARA**  
Two. The other’s flat. Wait. Hold the light lower.

*[F. A small scoop of water into the tin. The lid presses shut.]*

**IVO**  
Up you come. Have you got the rail?

**MARA**  
I’ve got you.

*[A breath as they steady themselves.]*

**IVO**  
Ready?

**MARA**  
Yes. Put it out.

*[G. The switch clicks off. Leave a beat of quiet.]*

### 2:18–2:44

**IVO**  
Concrete again.

**MARA**  
Here. Cup your hands. Keep it level.

*[H. The tin changes hands. A small tilt makes pebble and water move together.]*

**IVO**  
He used to make me take my boots off, too. Said I’d never hear a thing with them on.

**MARA**  
Did you?

**IVO**  
Once. I left my socks on. He laughed all the way back up the steps.

### 2:44–3:00

**MARA**  
Come round in the morning. Tell him that bit.

*[Allow a beat for Ivo’s answer.]*

**IVO**  
All right. You carry the tin. My hands are shaking.

*[I. A final small wash inside the tin. Let it settle into room tone. End at 3:00.]*
