More journeys per kilometre

In 1891 the tramway recorded 3.50 passenger journeys for every kilometre of service. By 1896 that figure had risen to 4.11. Passenger journeys grew by 37% over those five years, while service distance grew by 17%.

The dip in 1894

A bridge closed for six weeks that year. The tramway recorded 9,000 fewer journeys than in 1893 and ran 2,000 fewer kilometres. Both totals rose above their 1893 levels in 1895. The ledger has no daily entries, so we cannot tell how much of the dip occurred during the closure.
