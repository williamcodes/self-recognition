How scoring works

A/B

The reader selects its own work from two candidates. The headline averages accuracy across comparison writers, giving each writer equal weight.

Yes/no

The reader gets credit both for claiming its own work and for rejecting another model’s work. Balanced accuracy averages those two success rates.

Chance is 50% on both measures. A scenario means one underlying brief or interaction setup. Reversing A/B order or rejudging a scenario adds judgments without adding scenarios.
