# Call for Vendors: Cedar Loom Indoor Makers Market

**Saturday, November 21 · 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. · Cedar Loom Arts Center**

We have twelve tables for local makers selling their own ceramics, prints, textiles, or paper goods. First-time sellers are welcome.

**How to apply**

1. Pick up a paper application form at the reception desk.
2. Return it to the reception desk by 5 p.m. on October 30.

**What happens next**

We will contact every applicant by November 4. If we receive more eligible applications than we have tables, we will draw names at random.

**Cost**

The table fee is $15. You pay only after your table is confirmed, and applying is free.

Questions? Ask at the reception desk.
