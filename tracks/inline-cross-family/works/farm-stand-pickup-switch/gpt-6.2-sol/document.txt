# Foxglove Farm Stand  
## Saturday, August 8 · Prepaid Produce Box Pickup

Pick up your prepaid produce box from **9 a.m. to noon** at the **covered loading porch behind the stand**. The front lot will be hosting a pottery sale.

Enter from **Mill Lane** and follow the **green arrows** to the porch. Give the surname on your order at the porch table. If you’d like staff to bring your box to your car, ask at the table.

Any boxes unclaimed on Saturday can be picked up **Sunday from 10 a.m. to noon** at the usual counter.
