# Produce-Box Pickup Moves to the Back Porch on Saturday, August 8

**For prepaid produce-box customers**

On Saturday, August 8, pick up your box at the covered loading porch behind the stand. The front lot is hosting a pottery sale that day.

**Pickup hours are unchanged: 9 a.m. to noon.**

**How to find us**

1. Enter from Mill Lane.
2. Follow the green arrows to the porch.
3. Give your order surname at the porch table.

**Need a hand?** Ask at the table and staff will bring your box to your car.

**Can't make it Saturday?** Unclaimed boxes can be collected on Sunday, August 9, from 10 a.m. to noon at the usual counter.

Thank you for your patience, and enjoy the pottery sale while you're here.

*Foxglove Farm Stand*
