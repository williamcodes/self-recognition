# Alder Steps Library  
## Temporary book return · June 3–14

**Return books and magazines to the blue bin beside the courtyard entrance on Willow Lane.** You can use the bin any time, day or night.

The return slot here beside the front doors will be closed June 3–14 while the doors are repainted. For board games and other boxed items, please bring them to the library desk during staffed hours.

The library will keep its usual opening hours. **This slot reopens June 15.**
