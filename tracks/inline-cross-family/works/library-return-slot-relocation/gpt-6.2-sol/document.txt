# Temporary Book Return · June 3–14

## Use the blue bin beside the courtyard entrance on Willow Lane

You can return books and magazines in the blue bin 24 hours a day. The return slot beside the front doors will be closed June 3–14 while the doors are repainted.

Please bring board games and other boxed items to the library desk during staffed hours. Alder Steps Library will keep its usual opening hours.

**The front return slot reopens June 15.**
