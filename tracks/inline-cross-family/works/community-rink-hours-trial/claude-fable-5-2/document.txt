# Saturday Public Skate: Four-Week Time Trial

**Juniper Recreation Rink**

To skaters and caregivers:

For four Saturdays, September 5 through September 26, the public session will move from 2–3 p.m. to **4–5 p.m.** There will be no 2 p.m. public session on those dates.

**Why the change?** On four pilot Saturdays in July, we counted skaters at the door for both time slots. The 2 p.m. sessions drew 18, 22, 20, and 16 skaters, an average of 19. The 4 p.m. sessions drew 29, 31, 27, and 33, an average of 30. That is 11 more skaters per session in the later slot, so we are testing whether it serves more families.

**What stays the same:** Entry is $4 and skate rental is $2.

**Tell us what you think.** Feedback cards are available at the front desk. Your comments will help us decide what happens after the trial ends.
