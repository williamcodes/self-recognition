PUPPET MATINEE
Sharing and setup

BEFORE THE VISIT
Attach the Family Packet PDF to your usual pre-visit message or offer
it on the event page. Page 1 is for caregivers. Pages 2 and 3 are a
visual story families can read together.

Suggested message
“Here’s a short guide and a picture story you can read together before
the puppet matinee. They show what your child may see and hear and how
you can step out and come back during the show.”

AT THE SHOW
• Keep a few copies of the packet or visual story (pages 2 and 3) where
  families check in.
• Post the Side Door Sign next to the door guests use to step out and
  come back. Place it where families can see it from inside the room,
  clear of permanent exit signs.
• If a family asks about taking a break, point out the door and say,
  “You can step out and come back in through this door during the show.”

PRINTING
Both PDFs are US Letter, portrait, and work in black and white. Print
at actual size on Letter or choose Fit on A4. Print the sign single-sided.
