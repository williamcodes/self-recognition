# Mounting the courtyard bench vote

Print both pages of **Courtyard bench exhibit.pdf** single-sided on A3 paper, portrait, at actual size. Use uncoated paper so pencil marks show clearly. Print a spare copy of page 2.

Write the closing date and time on the voting sheet, two weeks after opening. Mount the shade survey on the left and the voting sheet on the right, with a 2 cm gap. The pair measures **61.4 cm wide × 42 cm high**, leaving about 14 cm on each side when centered on the 90 cm board. Place the voting circles within reach of seated residents. Attach a sharpened pencil on a cord long enough to reach every row.

Each location has 38 circles. If any row fills, save the marked sheet and put up the spare with the same closing date. At closing, count marked circles for each location across all sheets and post the three totals by the mailboxes.
