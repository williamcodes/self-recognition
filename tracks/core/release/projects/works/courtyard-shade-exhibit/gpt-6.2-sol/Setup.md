# Courtyard reading bench exhibit

## Print and mount

1. Print both pages of `Courtyard-exhibit.pdf` in color on separate A3 landscape sheets. Select **Actual size** or **100%**.
2. Hang the shade comparison on the left and the voting sheet on the right. With a 1 cm gap, the two sheets use **85 cm of width × 29.7 cm of height**, leaving 2.5 cm on each side of a 90 cm board when centered.
3. Fill in the opening and closing dates for the two-week vote. Attach a dark pen beside or below the voting sheet and mount it where residents can comfortably reach the circles.

## Run the vote

Each resident marks one empty circle beside their preferred location. The voting sheet holds 54 marks per location. If a row fills, write the same dates on a fresh copy of page 2, swap it in, and keep the original for counting.

At closing, take down the voting sheet and count one vote per marked circle. Add the counts for each location across all sheets used.
