    How the projections work

    Enrollment. Baseline uses the exact mean of four fictional historical sessions per format. The stress case subtracts one from each mean. Fractional averages are kept for projections.

    Direct costs. Session margin is attendance × (price − materials per attendee) − instructor cost. Term margin sums the six session margins. Break-even is the minimum whole-person attendance that covers those costs. Overhead is outside this calculation.
