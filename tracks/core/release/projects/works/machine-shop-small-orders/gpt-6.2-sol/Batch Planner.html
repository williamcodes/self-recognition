Labor assumptions

Labor = 4 minutes per piece + 25 minutes per scheduled run. Compare each week with 270 minutes. Spare time in other weeks cannot cover an overage within the same week.

Shipping assumptions

One run sends all orders Friday and moves zero earlier. Two runs assign orders received through Wednesday noon to Wednesday and the rest to Friday. Earlier orders include 10 in the over-capacity week and assume all scheduled work is completed. Midweek piece counts and labor availability are unknown, so confirm Wednesday readiness before committing.
