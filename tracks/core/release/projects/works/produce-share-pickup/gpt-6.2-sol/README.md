# Neighborhood produce share pickup kit

Print one copy of each PDF and use the kit in the lobby for a 64-household pickup. The member materials pair English and Spanish with matching pictures. Spanish is the second language used in this kit because no member language list was supplied. Volunteers can use the pictures and box signs with anyone who prefers to point.

| File | Print | Put it to work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Member signs](print/01-member-signs.pdf) | 3 sheets, 11 × 17 in, single-sided. Pages 1–2 portrait, page 3 landscape. | Place the entry and swap posters on the two stands. Cut the three signs on page 3 and lay them face up toward members or attach them to the table edge. |
| [Swap tickets](print/02-swap-tickets.pdf) | 1 sheet, 11 × 17 in, portrait. | Cut on the dashed grid for 64 tickets. Place the stack with the box volunteer. |
| [Volunteer kit](print/03-volunteer-kit.pdf) | 5 sheets, US Letter, single-sided. If the printer is loaded with 11 × 17 paper, print centered at 100%. | Read the first two pages before setup. Fill the two roster pages and the shift and count sheet before opening. Keep all five pages behind the table. |

Choose **Actual size / 100%** and auto-orient, or print the landscape sign page separately. Color is optional. The signs use words and distinct pictures so black-and-white copies still work. Set out a pen, scissors and the organizer’s current household list. Tape is optional.

The layout diagram assumes the entrance can also serve as the exit. Confirm the exit and queue space with center staff, then turn or mirror the layout to preserve a clear route and door swing. The kit uses three active roles across two shifts. The opening count, household assignments and pickup times are filled in on site.

The PDFs contain their fonts and graphics and can be opened and printed offline. The editable layout and artwork are in [source/make-kit.swift](source/make-kit.swift). To regenerate the PDFs on macOS with Swift installed, run `swift source/make-kit.swift` from this directory.
