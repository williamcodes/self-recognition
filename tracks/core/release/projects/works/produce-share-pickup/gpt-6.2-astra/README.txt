NEIGHBORHOOD PRODUCE SHARE
Pickup day kit

Open Print/Pickup day.pdf in a local PDF reader. Print one copy,
single-sided, at actual size on landscape 17 × 11 inch (tabloid) paper.
Color or grayscale works. Allow time to cut and fold the two size tents.
Bring scissors and a pen. A small piece of tape on each tent foot can
help it stay in place on a slick table.

1  Entry stand       Members can point to their box size here.
2  Swap stand        Place alongside the optional exchange area.
3  Two table tents   Cut on the dashed line. Fold on the dotted lines.
4  Setup guide       Use for the lobby layout and volunteer briefing.
5  Pickup tally      Share one sheet across both shifts. Circle one size
                     per box issued. Count the circled sizes at close.

The member signs use English and Spanish, with pictures and matching
shapes for pointing. Editable/ contains one self-contained SVG per page
for local language or layout changes. The PDF embeds its fonts.
