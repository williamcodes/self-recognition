Box office  /  Free matinees

Send the reminder one day before.

Use one day before as a trial default. Across eight fictional matinees, 81.6% of reservations turned into attendances with a one-day reminder, compared with 74.6% with a three-day reminder.

Does the choice of average matter?

The weighted rate gives each reservation equal weight by dividing total attendances by total reservations. The per-show rate gives each matinee equal weight by averaging its attendance percentage. Both favor the one-day timing.

Removing school holidays widens the gap

The second matinee in each group fell on a school holiday. Those two shows had similar attendance rates (67 of 80 and 68 of 82). Removing both leaves three shows per timing.

How much confidence to place in the decision

Staff availability determined reminder timing. If staffing varied with the day of the week or the audience a show attracted, those differences could explain some or all of the gap. Excluding holidays addresses only that one scheduling difference. Four shows per group give limited evidence about future matinees.

To test the decision, randomly assign reservations within each future matinee to receive their single reminder either one or three days before. Keep the wording and channel the same, and compare attendance within each show. Include only reservations made before either reminder is due.
