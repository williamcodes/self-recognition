SATURDAY BREAD  /  COUNTER KIT

Set up the counter
Open Ready to print/priced.pdf. Print single-sided on US Letter at
actual size (100%). Slide page 1 into the upright holder. Keep page 2
behind the counter on a clipboard. Print extra copies of page 2 as needed.
Write the full Saturday pickup date and sheet number before taking orders.

Take a preorder
Ask for the pickup name, items and quantities, and one pickup window.
Use one row per order. Use a surname or initial to distinguish similar
names. Leave unused quantity cells empty. Tick exactly one window.
Read the order and Saturday pickup time back to the customer.

Example: Lee orders one seeded loaf and a box of four buns for 8–10 a.m.
Write Lee, enter 1 under Seeded loaves and 1 under Bun boxes, then tick
8–10 a.m. The box counts as one unit. At the listed prices the total is $19.

At Friday noon, close that Saturday's sheet and total the three quantity
columns. Start a newly dated sheet for the following Saturday's orders.
On pickup, find the name and tick Picked up when handing over the order.

Change prices
By hand: print Ready to print/write-in.pdf. Fill all three white price
panels on the sign and the three price lines on the matching order sheet,
including dollar signs. Keep a clean master for future copies.

On a laptop: open Counter kit.html in a browser. Enter the three prices,
select the pages you need, then use Print / save PDF. A saved PDF keeps
the new prices for reprints. The editor opens with the original menu
each time. All files work offline.
