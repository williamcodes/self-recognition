How the rates are calculated

For each stop, add up the boardings, minutes and buses in the selected dates. Divide total boardings by total observed hours for the hourly rate, or by total buses for the per-bus rate. Every observed minute counts, including time spent waiting when no buses arrive. A dash means the rate has no denominator.
