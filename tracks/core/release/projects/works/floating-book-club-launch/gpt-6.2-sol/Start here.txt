BETWEEN CUPS
Book club launch kit

KICKOFF
Wednesday, March 10, 2027, 6:30–7:30 p.m.
March 10 is the second Wednesday of the next March.
The room has 16 seats total. The host uses one of them.

BEFORE YOU SHARE
1. Confirm the independent café, its street address, and the seating
   arrangement. Choose one RSVP contact or booking link.
2. Open Kickoff invitation.pdf in a PDF reader. Click the lines under
   CAFÉ, STREET ADDRESS, and RSVP TO RESERVE A SEAT. Enter the details
   and save a copy. If a browser preview won’t let you type, open the
   downloaded file in a desktop PDF reader. For a paper flyer, you can
   also handwrite the details.
3. To share a static PDF, print the completed copy to PDF using the
   print dialog. Reopen the result and check all three entries. Paste
   the completed Invite message.txt into email or a group chat so the
   details are also available as plain text.

PRINT AT HOME
• Kickoff invitation.pdf   One page. Print the completed flyer as needed.
• Host packet.pdf          Two pages. Print one copy, single-sided.
  Page 1 is the timed agenda. Page 2 is the RSVP and arrival list.
• Table signs.pdf          One page. Print one copy, single-sided.
  Cut along the dotted line. Set the welcome sign by check-in and
  the other sign on the table.

All pages are US Letter. Use Actual Size on Letter or Fit on A4.
The materials also work in grayscale. Print a proof before making copies.

MANAGE RESERVATIONS
Use the door list to count the host and any seats the café holds back
before confirming RSVPs. Keep overflow on a waitlist and only confirm
a seat once it’s available. Bring the list to check-in and keep it with
the host.
