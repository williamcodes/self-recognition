# Spoke House repair pickup kit

## Set up the counter

Print the [counter sign](print/counter-sign.pdf) single-sided at actual size on portrait US Letter paper (8.5 × 11 inches). Place it in the upright holder facing the queue. The sign shows an example so customers can see how parts and labor appear on their own record.

Print the [completed customer slips](print/customer-slip.pdf) for the example job or the [blank slips](print/blank-slip.pdf) for other jobs. Use the same paper and settings. Cut once across the dotted center line to make two half-letter slips. Keep the stack at the register behind the counter.

## At pickup

On a blank slip, enter each part or service on its own line, mark it as part or labor, and enter the charge. Confirm payment before filling in “Total paid.” Hand the customer their copy and use the slip to walk through the work.

For the example job, the cashier can say:

> We replaced your rear tube and cleaned your chain. The tube was $9, the flat repair was $18, and the chain cleaning was $12. You paid $39. Here’s your copy. If you have questions, ask the repair cashier here on weekdays from 4 to 6 p.m.

The [editable artwork](artwork/) contains one SVG for each print sheet. Keep both copies on a slip sheet in sync when editing.
