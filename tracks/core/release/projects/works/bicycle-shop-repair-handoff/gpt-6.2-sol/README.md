# Bicycle repair pickup kit

Print on US Letter (8½ × 11 in) at **Actual size / 100%**. Cut along the corner marks. The sign is sized for a **5 × 7 in portrait holder**. Keep the cut slips with the repair tickets or at the register.

| Print file | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Counter sign](01-counter-sign.pdf) | Place one sign in the upright holder, facing customers. |
| [Example handoff](02-example-handoff.pdf) | One completed 3¾ × 4¾ in customer slip for the sample repair. |
| [Blank pickup slips](03-blank-pickup-slips.pdf) | Four 3¾ × 4¾ in slips per sheet for future jobs. |

At pickup, match the customer to their repair ticket. Review each part or service and its charge, confirm the amount paid, and hand over the slip with the receipt. For future jobs, fill in the job number, date, completed work, charges, and total paid.

**Sample handoff**

> We replaced the rear tube. The tube was $9 and the rear flat repair was $18. We also cleaned the chain for $12. You paid $39 total. If you have questions, stop by on a weekday between 4 and 6 p.m. and ask for the repair team.
