How the comparison works

Monthly loans per copy equal the four-month loan total divided by four and then divided by the branch’s copy count. Each scenario holds both branches’ loan averages fixed and updates their copy counts for the proposed moves.

The combined gap adds the absolute difference between East and West for each title, weighting A, B and C equally. Change equals the scenario’s combined gap minus the current gap. Negative values show improvement. Ranking uses unrounded values, then favors fewer moves in a tie.

Only West-to-East moves are allowed, with a maximum of three copies moved and a minimum of two copies per title at each branch. The projections depend on loan demand staying at the historical averages.
