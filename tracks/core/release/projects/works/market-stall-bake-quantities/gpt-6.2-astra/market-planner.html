Selling the last item gives a lower bound on demand. The till records everyone who bought one and misses customers who arrived after it ran out. The extra-demand setting tests that uncertainty by adding units only to a product that sold out on that Saturday.

The replay treats the markets as equally likely, assumes customers keep their product choice, and gives leftovers zero revenue. Each muffin sells for $4.00 and costs $1.20 to bake. Each scone sells for $3.50 and costs $1.05. Contribution is before stall fees, labour and other overhead.
