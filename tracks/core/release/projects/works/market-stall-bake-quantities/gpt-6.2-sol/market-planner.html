How the planner scores a mix

Observed sales. Replay each market with the proposed quantities and use recorded sales as the demand proxy. Each product sells the smaller of its proposed bake quantity and that day’s recorded sales.

Sellout uplift. Use the same replay and add the chosen extra demand on positive-quantity sellouts only. Both scenarios keep muffins and scones paired by market and average the included markets equally. Markets where either product was unavailable are excluded from the mix replay, while historical totals include every row.

These are scenario averages from a small history. Weather, seasonality and changes in market traffic can shift demand. The uplift is an assumption for testing the bake plan’s sensitivity to missed sales.
