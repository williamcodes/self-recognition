Branch library seating

Where should four seats go?

Eight-week trial · Fictional counts from four consecutive Saturdays

Recommendation

Add all four seats by the windows

Raise window capacity from 6 to 10 seats. Window seating had the highest occupancy rate (95.8%) and was full in 6 of 8 observations, the most of any area.

How much of each area was used?

Quiet had the most occupied seats at every check. Window had the highest occupancy as a share of capacity.

What to track during the trial

Count occupied seats in each area at 11 a.m. and 2 p.m. each Saturday. Track how often more than six window seats are used. Separately tally patrons observed looking for a window seat when all ten are occupied. The historical counts did not measure unmet demand.
