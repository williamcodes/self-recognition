# Under One Roof

An eight-minute drop-in listening room about a fictional former indoor market. Each ticket opens a short conversation with a market worker. Both seats offer the same six tickets.

## Set up the room

- Print **print/Wall panel.pdf** as one A2 portrait sheet at actual size. Mount it where visitors coming through the entrance can see it and step aside to read. Leave a waiting spot beside the panel, outside the door swing and the path to both seats.
- Print **print/Reading sheet.pdf** double-sided on A4, flipping on the long edge. Put several loose copies in a holder beside the panel. Visitors can read all six interviews here, including anyone who prefers to skip the headphones.
- Place a screen and headphones at each seat, within sight of the waiting spot. Copy this whole project folder onto each station computer. Open **index.html** locally in a browser and use full screen. Keep the audio folder, scripts, illustration and stylesheet beside the page in their supplied locations.
- Try a ticket at each seat, press the audio play control and adjust the headphones to a comfortable starting volume. Select **All topics / finish turn** to stop playback and restore the chooser.

## A visit

Allow roughly one minute to browse the panel. Visitors can begin with any number, find the same ticket on either screen, and listen, pause, replay or read. Each recording lasts 30–34 seconds. With a seat free and nobody waiting, there is time to explore several in an eight-minute visit.

When both seats are in use, arriving visitors browse the reading copies beside the panel. When a seat opens, the person who arrived first takes it. Each seated listener finishes one interview and offers the seat if someone is waiting. Visitors who want another turn can rejoin the wait. The entrance also serves as the exit.
