# Under One Roof

A local history room about a former indoor market. Six fictional interviewees describe the work, favors and small mishaps that shaped a day in the hall, plus the annual winter fair.

## Set up the room

1. Print [the wall panel](print/wall-panel-A2.pdf) in color at A2 portrait (420 × 594 mm). Mount it on the inside wall beside the entrance so visitors can read it off to one side, with the doorway and route to both seats clear.
2. Print [the station labels](print/station-labels-letter.pdf) on one US Letter sheet and cut around the two cards. Put A at one seat and B at the other, where both letters can be seen from the panel. The letters identify seats. Each seat has the same six interviews in the same order.
3. Copy the entire [station folder](station/) to each station. Open [station/index.html](station/index.html) in a modern browser directly from the local files. Keep the folder intact so the recordings load. Connect headphones at each seat, set a comfortable volume, and leave the browser open to **Choose an interview**. The station runs offline and works with touch, mouse or keyboard.

## How a visit works

Visitors pick a topic at the wall and match its two-digit number at the next free seat. They can play the audio or open **Read transcript**. If both seats are occupied, visitors choose while they wait to one side and take whichever seat opens first. When someone is waiting, visitors give up the seat after one interview. **All interviews** stops playback and returns the screen to the topic list.

The recordings run 45–53 seconds each, about 4 minutes 53 seconds in total. Within a typical eight-minute visit there is time to orient, wait through a short turn, and listen. When seats are free, visitors can hear several interviews.

Before opening the room, try the same topic at both seats with the devices offline. Check that each headphone set plays at a comfortable level, the on-screen transcript opens, and a visitor can read the panel without blocking the doorway.
