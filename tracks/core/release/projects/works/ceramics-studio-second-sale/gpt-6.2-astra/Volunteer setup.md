# Loop Foot · Saturday market setup

Print **Market day print kit.pdf** single-sided on US Letter at **Actual size / 100%**, in black ink. White or light uncoated card keeps the signs readable and the write-in cards easy to mark. Plain paper clipped to cardboard also works.

Pack five sign holders or stiff backings, clips or removable tape, scissors and a broad black marker.

| Page | Placement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 · Entrance | Face arriving shoppers. Keep the hours visible above the pieces. |
| 2 · Small dishes | Rack 1 · $8 each |
| 3 · Planters | Rack 2 · $12 each · Decorative |
| 4 · Vases | Rack 3 · $18 each · Decorative |
| 5 · Pay here | Face the browsing area from the payment table. |
| 6 · Price cards | Keep behind the payment table for changes. |
| 7 · Piece labels | Print as many copies as needed. Each sheet has three labels of each kind. |

**Volunteer 1** sets the entrance sign and clips one category sign high on the front of each rack. Arrange the racks in number order if the room allows. Keep “Decorative” visible on both the planter and vase signs.

**Volunteer 2** sets the checkout sign and cuts the piece labels. Place the appropriate label beside each piece or clearly separated group. On cosmetic-second labels, tick each listed variation that applies. Leave space at the table for shoppers to set down their selections.

**Before 11 a.m.** Walk in together from the entrance. Check that each price reads clearly over the pottery and that the checkout sign is visible from the last rack.

**To change a price** write the new amount, including the dollar sign, on a blank page 6 card. Cut on the outline and tape the card over the old price box. Tell the volunteer at checkout. The box is 3½ × 1½ inches on every rack sign. Both pages must print at the same scale.

For future edits, open the matching file in **Editable signs** in an SVG editor. All lettering is live text. The print PDF embeds its fonts.
