## Saturday Public Skating Trial at Juniper Recreation Rink

Skaters and caregivers, please mark your calendars. From September 5 through September 26, the Saturday public skating session will move from 2–3 p.m. to 4–5 p.m. for a four-week trial.

Door counts from four July pilot Saturdays averaged 30 skaters at 4 p.m. and 19 at 2 p.m. That’s 11 more skaters per session on average, so we’re trying the later time for four consecutive Saturdays.

Entry remains $4, and skate rental is $2. Pick up a feedback card at the front desk to let us know how the later time works for you.
