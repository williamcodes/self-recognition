**Juniper Recreation Rink Public Notice**

Skaters and caregivers, please plan for a later Saturday skate. From September 5 through September 26, the Saturday public session will move from **2–3 p.m. to 4–5 p.m.** for a four-week trial.

On four pilot Saturdays in July, door counts averaged 19 skaters per session at 2 p.m. and 30 at 4 p.m. The later sessions drew 11 more skaters on average, so the rink is trying the later time this September.

Entry is $4, and skate rental is $2. Please use a feedback card at the front desk to tell us how the new time works for you.
