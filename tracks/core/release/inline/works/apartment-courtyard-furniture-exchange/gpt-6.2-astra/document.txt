# Tern Court Apartments Furniture Exchange

**October 10 · Shared courtyard**

**10:45 a.m.** — Drop-off opens.  
**11 a.m. to 1 p.m.** — Browse and take home an item for free.  
**1 p.m.** — Please collect any items you brought that are still there.

Have a stool, side table, or shelf that could use a new home? Bring up to two clean, usable small pieces that one person can carry. All residents are welcome to browse, even if you don’t bring anything.

**Rain location** — Ground-floor common room.
