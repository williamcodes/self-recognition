**Call for Vendors at Cedar Loom Arts Center**

Cedar Loom Arts Center invites local makers to sell their work at its indoor makers market on November 21 from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. If you make your own ceramics, prints, textiles, or paper goods, you can apply for one of 12 tables. First-time sellers are welcome.

Pick up a paper application at the center’s reception desk and return the completed form there by 5 p.m. on October 30.

If more than 12 eligible makers apply, the center will draw names at random. The center will contact applicants by November 4. The fee is $15 per table, payable after your table is confirmed.
