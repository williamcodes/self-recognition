# Call for Vendors | Cedar Loom Arts Center

Sell your work at our indoor makers market on November 21 from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. We have 12 tables for local makers selling ceramics, prints, textiles, or paper goods they’ve made themselves. First-time sellers are welcome.

The table fee is $15, payable after your spot is confirmed.

Pick up a paper application at the Cedar Loom Arts Center reception desk and return it there by 5 p.m. on October 30. If we receive more than 12 eligible applications, we’ll draw names at random. We’ll contact applicants by November 4.
