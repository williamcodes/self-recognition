**Foxglove Farm Stand**  
**Prepaid produce-box pickup moves to the back porch on Saturday, August 8**

The front lot will host a pottery sale on Saturday. You can pick up your prepaid produce box at the covered loading porch behind the stand during the usual hours of **9 a.m. to noon**.

Enter from **Mill Lane** and follow the green arrows to the porch table. Give staff the surname on your order. If you’d like someone to bring your box to your car, please ask at the table.

If you miss Saturday pickup, you can collect your box **Sunday from 10 a.m. to noon** at the usual counter.
