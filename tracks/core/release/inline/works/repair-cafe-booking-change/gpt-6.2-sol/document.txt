# Birch Room Mending Café
## April 18 booking change

**Reserve a slot in advance.** Visit the hall desk in person from April 6 through April 16 to reserve one free 20-minute slot.

**Come as a walk-in.** Three slots are held for visitors without a reservation. We’ll assign them in order of arrival, starting at 10 a.m. on April 18.
