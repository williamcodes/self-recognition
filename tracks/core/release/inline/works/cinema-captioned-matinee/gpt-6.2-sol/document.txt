## Kite House Cinema’s First Open-Captioned Matinee

**The Orchard Clock**  
**February 14 at 2 p.m.**

Join us for our first open-captioned matinee at Kite House Cinema. Captions will appear on the main screen throughout the film, displaying dialogue and relevant sounds. Everyone is welcome.

Seats are $6 and are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Tickets go on sale at the box office at noon on the day of the screening. The lobby opens at 1:15 p.m.
