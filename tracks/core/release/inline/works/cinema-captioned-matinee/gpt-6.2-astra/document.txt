**Kite House Cinema’s First Open-Captioned Matinee**  
*The Orchard Clock*  
**February 14 at 2 p.m.**

Everyone is welcome at this screening. Open captions appear on the main screen throughout the film, so everyone in the audience can read the dialogue and descriptions of relevant sounds.

Seats are $6 and are available first come, first served. You can buy tickets at the box office starting at noon on February 14. The lobby opens at 1:15 p.m.
