**Free Saturday Loop shuttle — temporary Depot stop move**

On May 2, 9, 16, and 23, board the free Saturday Loop shuttle at the bench outside the market entrance at **18 Clay Street**. The shuttle will use this stop in place of the regular Depot stop on all four Saturdays. Departures remain hourly from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.

In the fictional spring pilot, the market stop averaged 25 boardings per service day across four days. The regular Depot stop averaged 15 across four separate days, so the market stop averaged 10 more boardings per service day.

You can leave comments about the temporary stop with the driver.
