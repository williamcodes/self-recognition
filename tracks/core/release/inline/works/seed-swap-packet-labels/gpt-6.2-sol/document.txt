## Marigold Row Garden Club Seed Swap

**March 14, 10 a.m. to noon**  
**Oak Annex community room**

Come pick out seeds for your garden! First-time swappers are welcome. You can take home up to five packets, even if you don’t bring any seeds.

If you’d like to share, bring commercially packaged or home-saved vegetable or flower seeds. Please label each packet with the plant name and the year the seeds were collected or bought. Envelopes and pencils will be available at the volunteer table if you need to pack or label your seeds when you arrive.
