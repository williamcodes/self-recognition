**Marigold Row Garden Club Seed Swap**  
**March 14, 10 a.m. to noon**  
**Oak Annex community room**

First seed swap? Come pick out something you’d like to grow. Everyone can take up to five packets, even if you arrive without seeds.

If you have seeds to share, bring commercially packaged or home-saved vegetable or flower seeds. Please label each packet with the plant name and the year you collected or bought the seeds. The volunteer table will have envelopes and pencils so you can pack and label your seeds when you arrive.
