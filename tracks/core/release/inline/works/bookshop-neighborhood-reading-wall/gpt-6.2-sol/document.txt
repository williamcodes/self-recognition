# Help Fill Our Neighborhood Reading Wall

What book would you recommend to a neighbor? From July 1 to July 25, pick up a blank card here at the Driftwood Books counter. Write the book’s title and author, then add one short reason you’d recommend it.

Books of every genre and reading level are welcome. You can add your first name if you’d like. Children are welcome to tell a companion what to write on their card.

**Leave your completed card in the basket by the register.** Staff will display recommendations in the shop window through August 15, as space allows. Stop by to see what your neighbors are reading!
