**What should the neighborhood read next?**

From July 1 to July 25, help fill the Driftwood Books reading wall with books you love. Pick up a blank card here at the counter. Write the title and author of a book you recommend, then add one short reason a neighbor might enjoy it.

Books of every genre and reading level are welcome. Children may dictate their recommendation to a companion. Add your first name if you like.

Leave your completed card in the basket by the register. Our staff will display cards in the shop window through August 15 as space allows.
