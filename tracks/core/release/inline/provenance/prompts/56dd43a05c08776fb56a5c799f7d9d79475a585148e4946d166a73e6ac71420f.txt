# Weekday school visits pilot for pupils aged nine to eleven

The museum should pilot a 75-minute visit in which pupils use a paper timetable to make a journey decision, explain it to a partner, and then apply the same skill independently. The visit will connect that task to the museum’s collection by asking pupils to look at how a real timetable presented information to passengers. A ready-to-send teacher sheet and a short booking reminder will help schools prepare for the visit. The pilot will use the existing educator, volunteer, and two indoor rooms, with a budget of $2,700.

## The revised 75-minute visit

Set up one room for the collection activity and the other for seated timetable work. The class moves together once, with the educator leading and the volunteer supporting. For a class of 30, prepare six tables of five and 15 sets of pair materials. At each table, two pairs and one pupil working alongside a pair can share the first task, with roles changing between questions. Each pupil gets an individual exit card.

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| **0–8 minutes** | **Welcome and starting task.** The educator introduces the question, “How can a passenger use a printed timetable to plan a journey?” Pupils complete a short individual card using a simple timetable. The volunteer distributes and collects cards before any answers are discussed. |
| **8–20 minutes** | **Look at a collection timetable.** Using an enlarged reproduction of a timetable from the museum’s collection, the educator asks pupils to find the places, times, and direction of travel. Pupils point out what a passenger would need to know. The educator introduces the idea that one column can represent one vehicle’s journey. |
| **20–25 minutes** | **Move to the activity room.** The volunteer guides pupils to tables with the materials already laid out. |
| **25–38 minutes** | **Plan a journey together.** The educator models finding a boarding stop, reading across to a departure time, and following the same column to the destination. Pupils use a strip of card to track the column on their own copies. They explain how the departure and arrival times belong to the same service. |
| **38–58 minutes** | **Solve passenger challenges.** Pupils take turns tracing a journey and checking their partner’s explanation. They first find a service that arrives by a deadline, then tackle a journey with both a start time and a deadline. The educator asks groups to justify choices. The volunteer helps pupils orient the sheet, use the tracking strip, and take turns. |
| **58–69 minutes** | **Complete an individual exit task.** Each pupil uses a fresh timetable to choose a service and describe how they found the departure and arrival times. Adults can read the wording or record a pupil’s spoken answer where needed. Pupils make their own choices. |
| **69–75 minutes** | **Discuss and depart.** After collecting the cards, the educator shares two different ways passengers might choose a service. The volunteer gathers materials and helps the class leave on time. |

## Activity materials

Make an A3 reproduction of the collection timetable, an enlarged teaching timetable, 15 reusable timetable sets, and six table packs with pencils and blank working paper. Each reusable set contains a timetable, a card strip for following one column, and three passenger cards. Print in large, high-contrast type and use clear service headings so pupils can follow the timetable without relying on colour. Supply large-print individual cards and leave room for pupils to mark the route or circle times.

The following fictional timetable can be used for the guided practice.

**Museum bus. Weekday service toward the museum. All times are in the morning.**

| Stop | Service A | Service B | Service C | Service D |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Market Square | 9:00 | 9:20 | 9:40 | 10:00 |
| Station Road | 9:07 | 9:27 | 9:47 | 10:07 |
| Transport Museum | 9:15 | 9:35 | 9:55 | 10:15 |

- **Model together.** You’re at Market Square and can leave at 9:20. When does that bus reach the museum? Service B arrives at 9:35.
- **Passenger card 1.** You’re boarding at Station Road. What is the latest bus you can take to reach the museum by 10:00? Service C leaves at 9:47 and arrives at 9:55.
- **Passenger card 2.** You reach Market Square at 9:25 and must be at the museum by 10:00. Which service works? Service C leaves at 9:40 and arrives at 9:55.
- **Passenger card 3.** You reach Station Road at 9:50 and must be at the museum by 10:00. Can you make it on this timetable? The next departure is at 10:07 and arrives at 10:15.

Give pairs two prompts to use throughout the activity. “Show me where you found the departure” and “How do you know that arrival time belongs to the same bus?” Keep the model answers on the educator’s copy.
